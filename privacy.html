Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Creator Clip Studio (the "tool") is a personal desktop application for Windows, provided by Marceau PIGEOT as a private individual. It runs locally on the user's computer. This policy explains which data the tool uses, why, and where it is kept. In short: everything stays on your computer.

1. TikTok data we use and why

When you connect your TikTok account, the tool asks for two permissions (scopes) only:

ScopeData or accessWhy
user.info.basic Your open_id and your display name To show in the tool which TikTok account is connected.
video.upload Permission to send videos as drafts to your TikTok inbox To send the clips you choose as drafts. You then publish them yourself in the TikTok app.

The tool does not access any other TikTok data: no messages, no followers or followings, no videos list, no analytics, and no data about other TikTok users. It never publishes anything automatically.

2. Where data is stored

All data used by the tool, including the TikTok access and refresh tokens, is stored only on your own computer. The provider does not operate any server, online database or cloud storage for the tool, and never receives your tokens or your data.

3. Data sent to TikTok

The only data the tool sends to TikTok is the video files you choose to send, together with the information required by the official TikTok Content Posting API to upload them as drafts. This is done directly from your computer to TikTok.

4. Creators whose content is clipped

The tool does not collect personal data about the creators whose content is clipped, other than their public name (or public channel name). This name is kept in the local registry of authorized creators and is used to credit the creator in each clip's caption.

5. No sharing, no advertising, no tracking

  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • There is no advertising in the tool.
  • The tool uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking tools.

6. How long data is kept

Your TikTok connection data (open_id, display name and tokens) is kept on your computer only while your TikTok account is connected to the tool. When you disconnect, it is deleted from your computer.

7. Deleting data and revoking access

You can stop the tool's access to your TikTok account at any time, in two ways:

  • Click the Disconnect button in the tool. This deletes your TikTok tokens and account information from your computer.
  • Remove the tool's access in the TikTok app, in your account's security settings (the list of apps connected to your account).

8. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and the right to object to or restrict its processing. As the tool keeps your data only on your own computer, you can view or delete it directly. For any request or question, write to creatorclipstudio@outlook.fr; we will answer within one month. You can also file a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL, www.cnil.fr).

9. This website

This website is a simple static site. It does not collect any data: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages, under its own privacy policy.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page is updated.
