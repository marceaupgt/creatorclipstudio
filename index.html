Creator Clip Studio

Creator Clip Studio prepares short vertical clips from creators who have authorized clipping, and sends them as drafts to your TikTok inbox. You review, finish and publish every video yourself in the TikTok app.

How it works

  1. The creator authorizes clipping

    A streamer or YouTuber runs a paid clipping campaign and explicitly allows clippers to repost short clips of their videos.

  2. The tool prepares the clip locally

    On your own computer, the tool cuts the clip, formats it vertically, adds subtitles and writes a caption that credits the creator.

  3. The clip is sent as a draft

    Using the official TikTok Content Posting API (upload mode), the video is sent to your TikTok inbox. TikTok sends you a notification.

  4. You review and publish

    You open the TikTok app, check the video, edit it if needed and publish it yourself. Nothing is published without you.

Content and permissions

  • Authorized creators only. The tool is used only with content from creators (Twitch streamers, YouTubers) who have explicitly authorized clipping of their videos, as part of paid clipping campaigns they organize.
  • Registry of authorized creators. The user adds each authorized creator to a local registry. The tool refuses to process content from any creator who is not in this registry.
  • The creator is always credited. Every clip credits the original creator by name in its caption.

What this tool does not do

  • It never publishes automatically. Every video stays a draft until you publish it yourself in the TikTok app.
  • It does not access your messages, your followers, your followings or your analytics.
  • It does not access data of any other TikTok user.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data. There is no server, no online database, no advertising and no tracking.

The tool only uses two TikTok permissions: user.info.basic to show which account is connected, and video.upload to send drafts to your inbox. Your login tokens stay on your computer. You can disconnect at any time with the button in the tool, or remove access in your TikTok account settings. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
